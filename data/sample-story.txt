Asha, a tired ferry captain in her fifties, runs the last night crossing across a stormy bay. Her twelve-year-old son Kiran insists on riding along because his school trip was cancelled.

Halfway across, the engine fails in heavy rain. A smuggler named Rafe, who boarded with a locked metal case, pulls a knife and demands she turn the boat toward the old quarry dock.

Asha refuses. Rafe grabs Kiran. In the struggle on the wet deck, the case falls overboard and a flare ignites near the fuel drums. Asha fights the fire with an extinguisher while Kiran radios the coast guard.

At dawn, the coast guard cutter reaches them. Rafe is arrested on the pier. Asha watches the sunrise with Kiran, knowing she will have to tell the harbour board why she sailed into a storm.
